# Week 3, Day 5 — Tasks
## Wrap-Up & Presentation of Learning Outcomes (Lightweight — ~2 Hours)

Read today's Concepts doc first. Today is short — the goal is to consolidate Monday–Thursday into a short presentation of what was learned, using work you've already produced this week.

---

## Part 1 (~45 min) — Pull the Week Together
1. Gather your written summaries from Days 1–4 in one place.
2. Re-run both final models (Day 2's `LinearRegression` on insurance, Day 3's `LogisticRegression` on Titanic) just to confirm they still work end-to-end, and grab their final metrics fresh (RMSE/R² and accuracy/precision/recall).
3. Pick your single most illustrative moment from the week — a coefficient that told a clear story, a threshold change that shifted precision/recall in an interesting way, or a baseline comparison that put a score in perspective.

---

## Part 2 (~45 min) — Put Together a Short Walkthrough
Prepare a short walkthrough (a few slides, a short written page, or notes to talk through — whatever's lightest for you) covering:
1. The two problems this week (regression and classification), and how you knew which was which
2. Your final metrics for each, explained in plain language
3. The one illustrative moment you picked in Part 1
4. The single biggest habit or lesson you're taking forward (e.g., train/test discipline, matching metric to question, never trusting a score without a baseline)

Keep it short — this should be presentable in 5–10 minutes, not a formal report.

---

## Part 3 (~30 min) — Present and Reflect
1. Walk through it out loud, even if just to yourself or a peer — explaining it verbally surfaces gaps that reading your own notes silently doesn't.
2. Write two or three sentences on what you'd want to learn next if you kept going past this week (this feeds directly into Week 4's move toward tuning and feature engineering).

---

- [ ] Reconfirmed both models from the week still run and produce their final metrics

- [ ] Put together a short, presentable walkthrough of the week's work
